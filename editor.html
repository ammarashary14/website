Ready to share?

Your edits become public when you upload them to GitHub.

  1. Check your preview.

    Use Preview at the top to see the page with your latest edits.

  2. Download your page.

    Choose Download page. Your uploaded photos are included in index.html.

  3. Upload and commit on GitHub.

    Replace the existing index.html on the main branch. If your browser adds a number to the downloaded filename, rename it to index.html first.

    Open GitHub upload →

Only people with access to your GitHub repository can publish. Saving a browser draft does not change the live page. GitHub Pages usually updates shortly after your commit.
